Why "Max's"?

Max was the son of one of our founders. On August 7, 2023, we lost him to suicide. He was loved — deeply — by a family and a community that still carries him everywhere they go.

His dad and I served in the Marine Corps together and have been friends most of our lives. In the years since Max died, we kept coming back to the same conversation: the pain doesn't go away, but maybe it can go somewhere. Maybe it can become something that helps the next kid, the next family.

Max's Tees is that something. It's a small company with a simple job — put hope on people's backs, get the conversation started, and send real money to the people working on suicide awareness and prevention.

— The founders, Semper Fi
